ONEAQ Gmail MCP

Private, read-only mailbox connector for ONEAQ Holding B.V.

ONEAQ Gmail MCP is an internal tool used by ONEAQ Holding B.V. for its own bookkeeping. It runs locally on the company director's computer and gives an AI assistant read-only access to the company's own Gmail mailbox, so that incoming invoices and bank statements can be found and filed.

  • Access is limited to the Gmail read-only scope: the tool cannot send, delete or change email.
  • The tool is used only with the mailbox of ONEAQ Holding B.V. It is not offered to other users.
  • All data stays on the company's own computer.
